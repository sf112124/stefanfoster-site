From Earth to Sky

A fashion show in Cappadocia, under three hot air balloons designed as part of the collection. Les Benjamins × Apple.

Scroll

Two years ago we were briefed to make something for Les Benjamins and iPad. We thought Turkey. Then Cappadocia. Then, what if Benji designed three hot air balloons as part of his next collection?

We went to Cappadocia together, and he found his shapes in the valley’s wild flowers. From there we planned the show, the balloons and the collection as one thing.

The balloons were a feat of engineering and design. Nothing like them had been made before.
