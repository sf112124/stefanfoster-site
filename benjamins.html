From Earth to Sky

Les Benjamins × Apple. A fashion show in Cappadocia, under three hot air balloons Benji designed as part of the collection.

We were briefed to make something for Les Benjamins and iPad. We thought Turkey. Then Cappadocia. Then, what if Benji designed three hot air balloons as part of his next collection?

We went to Cappadocia together, and he found his shapes in the valley’s wild flowers. From there we planned the show, the balloons and the collection as one thing.

The balloons were a feat of engineering and design. Nothing like them had been made before.

Shot and shaped by director Neels Castillon.
